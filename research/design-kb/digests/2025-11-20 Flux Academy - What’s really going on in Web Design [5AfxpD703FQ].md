# What's really going on in Web Design
- Source: Flux Academy · 2025-11-20 · 31 min · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5AfxpD703FQ
- Speaker(s) and why they matter: Ran Segall, founder of Flux Academy, 20+ years as a designer, ex-freelancer and YouTuber. Sells web-design courses (Webflow, Framer), so he has a commercial interest in people entering web design; he says so himself.
- Relevance to us: 1 · Freshness matters? tool-dependent (market and tool landscape as of late 2025)
- Notes: unscripted, unedited rant, no design content. No sponsor reads. He mentions Webflow sponsored his channel early on and says he is not paid by Webflow or Framer now.

## Core thesis
Social media distorts the picture of web design: the market is what it always was (some do very well, some badly), income flexing has attracted people who care about money more than craft, and influencer tool recommendations are increasingly untrustworthy. His conclusion is that makers who love the craft will keep winning, and that with AI doing implementation, "the only thing left" for humans is being an artist.

## For 1000 Islands
Almost nothing actionable. The one transferable point: taste and craft stay the human contribution when AI does implementation, which supports spending our effort on direction, photography and copy rather than on tooling. Treat tool-war opinions from course sellers as biased.
