## Core thesis
Take references from places that are not just magazines, books or Pinterest, such as tarot decks, family books, fanzines, CDs, bookshop finds, and people around you; many small references stored in your head combine into original ideas.

## Claims and advice
- [REF] Get references from places that are not just magazines and books: "inspiration everywhere"; e.g. tarot decks whose identical card sets differ completely by illustrator. (0:06) — opinion
- [REF] A single page of one book gets stored in a "database" in your mind next to 50 other references, and that is where an original idea comes from. (2:08-3:08) — opinion
- [REF] Go to libraries and look at visual media that has not been digitised; nothing like it appears in your feed. (3:08) — opinion
- [REF] Scrolling online cannot compare to sitting with a creative person and seeing what they made and why (her friend's collage fanzine made of household rubbish). (3:08-4:08) — opinion
- [REF][VIS-C] Take colour palettes from unexpected places (a random French book) instead of Pinterest. (4:08-5:09) — practice
- [VIS-T] An "ugly" italic serif can work very well, depending on context (French book cover). (5:09) — opinion
- [VIS-L] In editorial work you can mix formats: Querida's magazine ends on glossy pages, using a completely different paper. (1:07-2:08) — opinion
- [VIS-L][EMO] Playful interaction of words with illustrations in a children's book (Pippi Longstocking edition) showed her text need not be conventional; still her style inspiration. (2:08) — opinion
- [EMO] She likes work "with heart", hand-made or hand-drawn (a minimal Jockstrap cover); respects work she cannot figure out how to make (Amaia's optical-illusion cover) because it makes her want to learn. (6:10-7:11) — opinion
- [REF][EMO] Do not limit yourself by identity: art need not say one thing about who you are; many outputs are fine (her 3D-modelled, printed and painted figurines were a way to pour accumulated references into one output). (7:11) — opinion

## Disagreements and contrarian points

## For 1000 Islands
Supports building a reference library for travel from non-web sources: local print, old postcards, boat and ferry tickets, regional signs, a local artist's work, paper textures and maps of the Thousand Islands, plus palettes pulled from real objects instead of Pinterest. Low priority beyond that.
