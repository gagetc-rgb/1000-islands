## Core thesis
The web went from reckless Flash-era creativity to a market where everyone copies the same parallax, colors and templates; to stand out, build each project around one simple, memorable idea (the "money idea", the project you can describe as "the one with...") that creates an emotional connection, then give it personality with layering, motion, sound and craft, balancing intuition with data.

## Techniques and steps
1. Find the "money idea": one sentence that finishes "the one with...".
2. Choose personality tools (copy, tone, illustration, motion, sound) deliberately and consistently.
3. Prototype in code (MatCap transitions, scroll story) to test a feeling early.
4. Add depth by layering: illustration, overlap of text and image, background grid or map, variable fonts, video loop.
5. Use scroll storytelling without hijacking the scroll.
6. Simulate before building physical work; test at odd hours and on site.

## Disagreements and contrarian points
Against the sameness of post-Flash, post-Dribbble web design and template reuse. Against scroll hijacking. Against relying on numbers alone: designers must trust intuition. Pro-emotional memorability as the primary goal, above process polish.

## For 1000 Islands
Direct: write the "money idea" for the site, one sentence, for the owner to say aloud ("the one with..."), and test every hero variant against it. Avoid the common travel-site look (parallax outdoors, same palette); use layering and a single strong visual world instead. Scroll storytelling is possible but no hijack. Cases are bigger-budget than our offer-minimum scope; take the principle, not the production.
