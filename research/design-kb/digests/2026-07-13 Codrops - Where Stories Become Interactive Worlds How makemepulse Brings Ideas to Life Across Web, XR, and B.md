## Core thesis
The studio says its edge is breadth plus craft: it picks the right format for each idea (website, WebXR, installation, game) and then makes it feel intuitive, beautiful and alive. Its stated belief is that the most interesting ideas rarely fit one category, and that digital can be a space for emotion and imagination rather than only a communication channel.

## Claims and advice
- [EMO] Heritage can be made tangible through play: for Audemars Piguet's 150th they skipped a commemorative site and built "The House of Wonders", 3D rooms modelled on cabinets of curiosity (quizzes, watch-assembly games, an AR portal, an alchemist's lab). — type: practice (self-described)
- [VIS-M][EMO] Detail echoes the brand: tiny mechanical elements, hidden interactions and refined transitions mirror watchmaking precision. — type: practice
- [EMO][Q] Reward curiosity to bring people back: 25+ pieces of "snackable content" and a badge collection system in the Audemars Piguet project, meant to encourage repeat visits. — type: practice
- [EMO] Make absence visible: UNESCO virtual museum of stolen cultural objects; 248 objects from 50 countries, many rebuilt from fragmentary documentation (sometimes a single low-quality image), objects floating free of vitrines, visitors create and share collections. Concept inspired by Francis Kere's baobab as a place of memory and gathering. — type: practice
- [AI][Q] Hybrid reconstruction workflow: AI-assisted reconstruction + expert 3D craft + UNESCO knowledge + "ethical transparency", so models show both what is known and what is unknown. — type: practice
- [EMO] Narrative from one simple belief: For Honor "Scars" is built on "every mark left by combat tells a story"; three warriors staged as an interactive standoff using real game models, with codes to unlock weapons in-game. — type: practice
- [VIS-M][Q] Bring cinematic quality to web and mobile without losing detail, using an in-house WebGL engine (NanoGL) so it runs across devices. — type: practice (tool-dependent)
- [EMO][REF] Personal project as a lab: "Nomadic Tribe", an original four-chapter interactive tale inspired by Moebius (Jean Giraud) and animation; own story, own soundtrack, 3D with comic-book visual codes; the studio says it won Site of the Year at both Awwwards and The FWA. — type: evidence (self-reported)
- [BIZ] Culture: work from wherever you are most effective, autonomy, and time for internal projects, prototypes and research beyond client briefs, used to test tools before client work. — type: opinion

## Disagreements and contrarian points
Against the "traditional commemorative website": heritage as game-like rooms. Against one-size-fits-all formats. No explicit critique of other studios.

## For 1000 Islands
Mostly out of scope (heavy 3D, installations). Transferable: (1) one organizing metaphor per site (cabinet of curiosities) gives a place a memorable structure; a quiet "collect what you saw" element could suit a multi-island region, but only if it fits the offer-minimum scope. (2) Details that echo the subject (water, ferries, granite) beat decoration. Do not copy the WebGL ambition; the budget and audience do not need it.
