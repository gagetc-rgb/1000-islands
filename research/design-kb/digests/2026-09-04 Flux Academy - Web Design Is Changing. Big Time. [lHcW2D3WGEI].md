## Core thesis
AI tools are collapsing the "zero to one" build step, so the value in web work moves to two ends: design (brand, positioning, messaging, aesthetic) and post-launch operations (management, marketing, conversion). Pure builders must pivot; designers should double down on fundamentals and brand.

## Claims and advice
- [BIZ][AI] Webflow showed a slide that AI tools solve "zero to one", and announced "Source": download a site (from Webflow or GitHub code) and manage it with specialized agents (copywriting, conversion, SEO), tying into email, CRM and Slack; pivot from a build platform to a marketing platform aimed at marketers. (0:00-2:01) — evidence (reported), tool-dependent
- [BIZ] Webflow's co-founder started a similar company ("Ploy", verify) that also imports and manages websites. (2:01) — evidence (reported)
- [AI][P] The new workflow (Flux's April video): Figma design, then Claude Code builds it, then deploy on Vercel or elsewhere; students report "completely transformed our margins" and being booked through October. (2:01-3:02) — evidence (testimonial, self-reported)
- [BIZ][Q] Three value phases: design (brand, positioning, messaging, structure), development (now becoming "commodified" by AI), and operations after launch (managing, marketing, traffic, conversion). Value moves to design and operations. (3:02-5:05) — opinion
- [BIZ] Framer is doubling down on design (design pages, competing with Figma); Webflow is aiming at marketing operations (competing with HubSpot-like platforms); Figma Sites also moves into development. The old Framer-vs-Webflow question fades. (5:05-7:08) — opinion
- [BIZ][AIS] Advice to designers who did design plus development: "double down on design", with strong fundamentals, brand voice and unique aesthetics, to stand out beyond "generic, AI slop average that looks the same". (7:08) — opinion
- [BIZ] Developers-only service providers must pivot to design or to "management and operations" (his preferred term over "maintenance"); an example agency rebranded from Webflow enterprise agency to a "web ops" company to sell long retainers. (7:08-9:11) — opinion/anecdote
- [BIZ] Can also do both: build the brand, then serve the client long-term. (9:11) — opinion
- [BIZ][P] Personal take: he likes strategy and design; building divs and classes was never the pleasurable part; AI lets him "go back to my roots". Real value lies in creating a brand or driving results (CRO, SEO, ongoing assets), not in technical development which only paid because it was hard. (9:11-11:13) — opinion
