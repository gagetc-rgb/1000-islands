## Core thesis
The studio says it turns abstract ideas, hidden stories and complex subjects into experiences people can explore and remember, combining strategy, design and technology. Its repeated pattern: take a dense subject (geology, a sobering report, ocean science, an architecture portfolio) and replace the conventional presentation with an interactive, narrative or spatial form.

## Claims and advice
- [EMO] Do not just explain, immerse. For a Bordeaux chateau ("Symphony of Vines") they had visitors travel back millions of years through the geology of the terroir, using abstract visuals, motion and sound, rather than explaining it "as the industry norm". — type: practice (self-described)
- [EMO] Turn a dry report into a world: RSPCA "Animal Futures" shows five possible futures for animal welfare as miniature 3D worlds, with interactive companions, collectible rewards and shareable moments. — type: practice
- [VIS-M][EMO] Let users move at their own pace and discover rather than read linearly. — type: practice
- [EMO][VIS-I] A narrator's voice as a draw: "The Sea We Breathe" for Blue Marine Foundation, narrated by Stephen Fry, three stories (sustainable fishing, blue carbon habitats, marine protected areas); later VR installations narrated by Helena Bonham Carter. — type: practice
- [BIZ] One project can grow into a long-term partnership (here: multilingual versions, VR installations, AR campaigns) "built on trust, shared ambition". — type: opinion
- [VIS-L][VIS-M] Replace the scrolling gallery with a physical metaphor: CLOU architecture portfolio uses a bespoke 3D project ring that doubles as data visualisation (scale, sector, status, scope). — type: practice
- [Q][VIS-L] Balance brand and function: Hubtown (Mumbai real estate) moves from a cinematic scroll introduction (vision, values, approach) to an interactive map of Mumbai for finding developments. — type: practice
- [P][BIZ] Even small briefs can be experimental opportunities (Solare typeface launch site for Nikolas Type, a "digital oasis" with interaction, motion and play). — type: opinion
- [VIS-M] Cosmos launch: storytelling-led marketing site with purposeful animation, plus marketing assets, refined onboarding and a custom invitation system with WebGL-generated 3D gemstones, so every touchpoint feels cohesive. — type: practice
- [BIZ] Culture claim: people matter more than work; trust, support, room to grow. — type: opinion
