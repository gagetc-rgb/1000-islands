## Core thesis
The studio positions itself as a dedicated partner for mission-driven startups and pioneers: "great design is the baseline, not the goal", the real work is understanding a mission well enough to move it forward. It claims to care about details most do not.

## Claims and advice
- [BIZ] Early days: said yes to nearly everything and treated each project as if the whole studio depended on it; "we don't get interesting projects, we make projects interesting." — type: opinion
- [P][BIZ] Understand the client's mission before designing; design is table stakes. — type: opinion
- [EMO][VIS-T] Psyop: for a studio whose logo carried the brand and whose work was buried behind a director roster, they took reference from title sequences of directors like Scorsese and Tarantino, where type is treated as seriously as anything on screen, to build an identity "expressive enough to hold its own and restrained enough to let the work lead". — type: practice
- [Q][VIS-L] Psyop site: turned a buried portfolio into something searchable, filterable and shareable via a single link, "a sales tool that never looks like one". — type: practice
- [EMO][VIS-M] ElevenLabs Store (NY Tech Week): a merch capsule plus a store you can talk to, order by voice and haggle; sharp, playful, technical but not cold. — type: practice
- [EMO][VIS-M] Perplexity Comet: product still a concept (no demo or wireframes), so they "designed the possibility rather than the product": cosmic theme, planets as vessels, parabolic lines from orbital paths, typography that expands on hover, page descends from space to Earth while the video travels the other way. — type: practice
- [EMO][VIS-M] Esther (Colombian rum): brand of duality and reflection, bright green cap; "wrote new rules as we designed" for how each element moves online. — type: practice
- [BIZ] Team of former educators, artists, scientists, engineers, ministers; disagreement is allowed to reach "the non-obvious". — type: opinion
- [P] "Brutal elegance": be honest about what matters, drop good ideas for one great one, make things exactly as they should be, "no more and no less". — type: opinion
- [BIZ] Principle seven, "Rally Others On": talk each other up, assume good intentions. — type: opinion

## For 1000 Islands
Useful: (1) "drop good ideas for one great one" fits the offer-minimum scope; (2) take a reference from an adjacent field (title sequences) rather than from other travel sites; (3) Psyop's lesson that a portfolio-like content set must be searchable and filterable maps to organising many islands, tours or stays; (4) Lenis is a possible tool for smooth scrolling, but check it against the "no motion that interferes" rule before using.
