The audience is product and app design, not marketing or travel sites.

## Core thesis
Metalab sells itself as a product-design partner behind well-known tech brands (Slack, Google, Robinhood, Nike, Uber, Midjourney, Headspace). The showcase argues that tight, single-client teams, brand plus product plus engineering under one roof, and a lean, remote, founder-minded culture produce products "thoughtful, functional, and built to last".

## Claims and advice
- [BIZ][P] Team structure: designers, engineers, researchers, product strategists and project managers; teams "always 100% dedicated to a single client so they can go deep"; everyone hands-on regardless of seniority. — practice
- [BIZ] Fully remote since day one: no office hours beyond a small overlap with clients, no timesheets; "do great work and hit your deadlines". — practice
- [P][DS] Suno (AI prompt-to-song): started as a brand engagement and grew to brand, product design and engineering; designed the three core prompts, visual language and Discover / For You pages; claimed #11 in Music on the Apple App Store under 24 hours after launch. — evidence (self-reported)
- [P][DS] Windsurf (AI coding tools): "flexible brand system built to scale across marketing site, product, and beyond"; balance of "structure and expression"; claimed acquisition deals ($2.4 billion Google, Cognition) "just months after launch", an implicit credit-claiming that should not be read as causal. — evidence (weak)
- [EMO][P] Atoms (official Atomic Habits app with James Clear): productized the Four Laws of Behavior Change (make it obvious, attractive, easy, satisfying) into the app; claimed top 10 in the App Store within a month and Apple Editors' Choice. Shows designing the product around an existing framework. — evidence (self-reported)

## Techniques and steps
No explicit method is given. Implicit pattern: start with brand work, expand to product and engineering once trust exists (Suno); build a brand system that spans marketing site and product (Windsurf).

## For 1000 Islands
Very little. One transferable point: single-focus, hands-on work for one client rather than juggling many, which mirrors our small-scope rule. Their approach (product design, large-scale systems, App Store performance) does not map to a photo-led travel site. Do not copy the proof style of impressive-but-unverifiable numbers; for our own site use verifiable guest-facing proof (reviews, photos, real numbers).
