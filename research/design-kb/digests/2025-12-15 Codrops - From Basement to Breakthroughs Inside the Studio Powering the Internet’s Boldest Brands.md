## Core thesis
basement positions itself as a boutique studio where branding, design, development, 3D and animation work together ("not in silos") for ambitious startups. Their showcase argues that a brand should look and behave like the product it represents (fast for devs, calm for a slow-tech product, handcrafted for an artist's store), and that internal "Labs" time is where the best projects start.

## Claims and advice
- [BIZ] Founded about 2018 when two designers merged their independent studios; now 40+ people spread over Mar del Plata, Buenos Aires and other cities; "boutique team with global reach". Claim: "Most of our clients come to us for one project... and stay for three." — evidence (self-reported)
- [P][BIZ] Culture rituals: annual retreats; weekly Labs "to explore new tools and ideas, many of our best projects started there"; they built their own pixel-art game, typefaces and Pixar-style shorts "No one asked." — practice (described)
- [EMO][VIS-T] Vercel: long-term partner; basement shaped visual language "from brand identity and custom type to web experiences and IRL events" and Next.js Conf (brand, site, motion, stage, live production). — practice
- [EMO][Q] Daylight (calm hardware product): brief was a site that "feels less like tech and more like touch", philosophy "slow technology for a fast world"; claimed sold out in hours. The site's tone mirrors the product's philosophy. — evidence (self-reported)
- [VIS-I][EMO] KidSuper World: e-commerce reimagined as "an artist's atelier"; real acrylics, watercolors, textures and sketches digitized into a 3D world, "handcrafted yet fluid"; won a Webby People's Voice Award (claim). Pattern: physical, analog materials scanned as the base of a digital identity for authenticity. — practice
- [VIS-L][P] Speakeasy (developer tools): modular, minimal identity "built to perform, not to decorate"; co-created in Argentina during two weeks of on-site collaboration with the client. A short on-site workshop as a process device. — practice
- [VIS-M][AI][DS] ElevenLabs: visual language built on Chladni patterns and the physics of sound; motion, blur and geometry "pulse like a voice in motion"; a custom generator turns data into art so the client team can "remix their own brand in real time". A brand system delivered as a tool, not only as static assets. — practice (tool-dependent)

## Techniques and steps
1. Take the product's own physical or philosophical truth (sound physics, slow technology, an artist's atelier) as the source of the visual language.
2. Digitize real analog materials (paint, textures, sketches) for authenticity.
3. Run an on-site co-creation sprint with the client (Speakeasy: two weeks).
4. Deliver brand systems that the client can extend (modular identity, custom generator).

## For 1000 Islands
Concept: tone should mirror the thing sold; Daylight's "calm, precise, built to last" is a good template for describing a quiet, upper-middle travel brand (slow travel for a fast world is not ours to copy, but the method is). Digitized analog material (paper, maps, hand-drawn route lines, local textiles) could give the photo-led site some authenticity without 3D. Most work shown is for tech startups and heavy dev; little on photography. Ignore valuations and award counts.
