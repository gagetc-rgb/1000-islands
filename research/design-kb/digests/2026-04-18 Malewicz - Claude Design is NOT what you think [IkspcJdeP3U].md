## Core thesis
Claude Design is Claude Code with spec files and an inspector, outputting React; it is a Canva replacement for non-designers, not a Figma killer or designer replacement. Because AI lifts everyone's baseline, average design stops being a differentiator, so designers who actually care and use AI to make things better rather than faster will be in demand.

## Disagreements and contrarian points
Against "designers are cooked" and "Figma killer" narratives; against the framing that Claude Design is a new product; against using AI mainly for speed. Contrasts with enthusiasts like DesignCode who see AI slop as fixable with references; here it is about raising the floor, so differentiation moves to taste and craft.

## For 1000 Islands
Little tool value. The strategic point is useful: since average AI-made travel sites will be everywhere, our differentiation must come from owned photography, specific copy and taste, not from the tool. His checklist for judging a design (contrast, spacing, a CTA, consistent depth) is usable as a quick review pass on our hero variants.
