## Techniques and steps
1. Derive the mood board from the product's own metaphor (Optikka: grids, libraries, archives, folders), then add a second set of references for movement (motion paths, tunnels, waves).
2. Anchor the tone in a specific era or subculture (Gentle Rain: early-80s West Coast).
3. Give the hero one signature effect that makes the brand name literal (water for "Gentle Rain"); bring in a specialist developer if realism matters.
4. Treat self-initiated projects (Motion.ed, the 7-year site) as portfolio and lead generation.

## For 1000 Islands
The usable idea is the concept-first method: pick a metaphor or an era that carries the brand (for us: water, islands, light, a local craft, not generic travel clichés), and give the hero one memorable signature element. The Optikka mood board logic (primary metaphor + movement layer) fits our Stage 2 mood board. The heavy 3D and WebGL work is out of scope for an offer-minimum site and would hurt load and mobile performance on a photo-led page. Treat the awards and "100,000 users" as marketing.
