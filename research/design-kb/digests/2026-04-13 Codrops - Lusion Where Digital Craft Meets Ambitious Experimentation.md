# Lusion: Where Digital Craft Meets Ambitious Experimentation
- Source: Codrops "Studio Spotlight" article · 2026-04-13 · article, ~960 words · https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/04/13/lusion-where-digital-craft-meets-ambitious-experimentation/
- Speaker(s) and why they matter: Lusion, digital production studio in Bristol, founded 2017 by Edan Kwan (self-taught designer and coder, originally from Hong Kong). Known for WebGL and real-time work; self-promotion, sells interactive and film production.
- Relevance to us: 2 · Freshness matters? tool-dependent (WebGL, generative imagery), the R&D-time idea is timeless
- Notes: no timestamps (article). Claims of "Site of the Year" from FWA, Awwwards and CSSDA and honours from Cannes Lions, D&AD, Webby are the studio's own. Tone is deliberately casual and self-deprecating.

## Core thesis
Lusion builds everything from scratch ("the best ideas usually fall apart the moment you force them into a template"), gives every project its own system, and funds experimentation through internal Labs projects that later win client work. The showcase is a satirical campaign, a CG film and a WebGL storybook, all treated with the same craft as a real brand job.

## Claims and advice
- [P][AIS] No templates: "every project gets its own system, its own logic, and its own flavour"; work ranges from polished and cinematic to "weird on purpose". — opinion
- [BIZ] Founder path: music, then self-taught design and code, freelancing, an agency role in New York, back to independent; studio site won Site of the Year (FWA, Awwwards, CSSDA); clients include Coca-Cola, Porsche, Max Mara, Google (their claim). — evidence (self-reported)
- [EMO][AIS] Oryzo AI: a satirical launch campaign for a fictional AI-era product (a cork coaster) presented with premium production and "complete seriousness"; delivered as a full campaign world (immersive web, 3D, motion, social posts, founder video, Product Hunt page, open-weight GitHub page). Point: an absurd idea treated with real craft shows production quality, and doubles as satire of AI-era launch conventions. — practice
- [P][AI] Porsche: Dream Machine: Wallpaper* connected them with Porsche GB, who had seen their immersive R&D work (Infinite Passerella); brief was a 2-3 minute CG short about Ferry Porsche's dream; they had never made such a film, said yes and "figured it out through making" in an intense 3-week production using generative imagery and motion design, moving from abstraction towards the silhouette of the original 356; shown by Wallpaper*, Porsche channels and Outernet London. — evidence (self-reported). Shows R&D work generating client briefs.
- [EMO][VIS-M] My Little Storybook: monthly-experiment project, WebGL plus handcrafted 3D environments and illustrated animation, a bird family crossing a river, inspired by Japanese anime; chosen deliberately to leave the photoreal commercial look and test a stylised language; built in one month. — practice
- [BIZ][P] Lusion Labs: time invested alongside client work for internal projects "to test ideas, develop new skills, and experiment creatively"; attracts people who want to keep learning. Close-knit team keeps communication simple. — practice

## Techniques and steps
1. Reserve a recurring internal experiment slot (monthly) with a one-month deadline.
2. Treat a joke or fictional product with full production standards, and publish supporting channels.
3. Take unfamiliar formats by building and learning in parallel when the opportunity is strong (3-week film).
4. Use R&D pieces as the portfolio that attracts the next client brief.
