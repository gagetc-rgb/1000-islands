## Core thesis
Obys argues that "big work" comes from the weight of ideas, not team size: a compact, hands-on team, a graphic-design foundation (typography, grid, hierarchy, composition) carried onto the web, and storytelling as the invisible structure of every site. Their view: freedom without structure is noise; the future is "clarity, responsibility", not louder visuals or more effects.

## Claims and advice
- [P][BIZ] Self-made path: seven years ago they built their own briefs (experimental sites, educational projects, concept platforms) and treated side projects with client-level discipline; awards came before big clients, so clients arrived already knowing what the studio stands for. — practice (self-described)
- [VIS-T][VIS-L] Graphic design mindset: typography, grid systems, composition, hierarchy carried into web; "the web is not a playground of random effects", it is "a structured environment where storytelling lives inside systems". — opinion
- [VIS-L][VIS-T] "You can break the grid only if you understand it. You can distort typography only if you respect it." Experiment responsibly. — opinion
- [EMO][P] Start every project by "understanding the story behind the project"; approach websites like editors approach long-form publications: tension, contrast, and "silence between strong statements"; layers that reveal gradually. — practice
- [VIS-L][Q] Glyphic Biotechnologies (protein sequencing by expansion): goal was to make a hard technology understandable, not to look innovative. Built as a "narrative system": dense visual sections alternate with calm readable blocks; PNG-sequence animations respond to scroll so users move through the explanation at their own pace. — practice
- [VIS-L][EMO] Grids (internal manifesto site, Site of the Month on Awwwards and Red Dot Best of the Best, their claim): "Structure is emotional. It creates rhythm, tension, and balance. It gives content authority." Disagrees with treating structure as invisible. — opinion
- [EMO][VIS-L] MAKHNO (architecture studio), concept "Art of Home" taken literally: entry screen is a house with three windows, three digital spaces under one roof; had to combine e-commerce and a corporate site into one ecosystem, unified but clearly separated; aim was a "tactile, almost physical" feel reflecting craftsmanship. — practice
- [BIZ][REF] Side projects (Obys Design Books, a curated platform for design books, quiet and focused on typography and grid) "keep us honest", remove external expectations, and teaching "forces clarity" that returns to commercial work. — opinion
- [AI][AIS] NLC (New Layer Capital): brand was bold, bright and loud (not their usual restrained style), and no photography, illustration or assets existed. AI was used "not as a generator of ideas": ideas, narrative structure and visual direction were theirs, AI extended them into visual material faster than traditional production. "Without a clear system and narrative, AI output is just noise." — practice/opinion (tool-dependent; tool not named)
- [BIZ][P] Staying small on purpose: faster decisions, deeper discussions, shared responsibility; designers understand development, developers understand motion, everyone understands the concept. They choose projects carefully. — practice

## Techniques and steps
1. Define the story first; derive the system (grid, type, pacing) from it.
2. Alternate dense visual sections with calm, readable blocks for complex topics; let scroll control the explanation pace.
3. Translate the concept literally into the interface (house with three windows) when the client has a strong identity.
4. Use AI only after concept, narrative and visual direction are set, to extend assets when none exist.
5. Run self-initiated projects with client-level standards to build credibility before big clients arrive.

## Disagreements and contrarian points
Against the idea that structure should be invisible or is restrictive; against effect-driven web design ("not a playground of random effects"); against AI as a source of ideas; against treating a studio as a service shop waiting for briefs.

## For 1000 Islands
High value for craft thinking: (1) set grid and type rules first, then break them deliberately; (2) editorial pacing, strong image moments followed by calm text, suits a photo-led travel site; (3) translate one concept literally into the interface (islands, windows, water, a map you move through); (4) when asset gaps exist, use AI to extend a clear art direction, never to invent the direction, and keep real photography for trust; (5) their small-team, high-care stance matches our offer-minimum scope. Scroll-driven PNG sequences are optional and costly on mobile; use sparingly.
