# Cross-check 1: UX and visual UI (NotebookLM, 11 Oct 2026)

Sources: 11 videos, authors in en, es, fr, ru (Todaro, Soy Dalto, Gianni Polito, Nielsen, Kuzmin/UPROCK, Schoger, Showalter, Sajid, Chainlift, Varfolomeev). Attribution is by the notebook's model, not yet spot-checked against transcripts (status: unverified).

## Agreed by 3+ authors (treat as best practice)
1. Proximity: group related elements closer together (Chainlift, Showalter, Sajid, Todaro, Polito).
2. Visual hierarchy through size, weight and contrast (Showalter, Sajid, Schoger, Polito).
3. Von Restorff / isolation: make the key element visibly distinct (Todaro, Soy Dalto, Polito, Schoger).
4. Fitts's law: primary targets easy to reach and large enough (Todaro, Soy Dalto, Polito).
5. Hick's law: fewer choices, simpler steps (Todaro, Soy Dalto, Polito).
6. Jakob's law: use familiar patterns and mental models (Todaro, Soy Dalto, Kuzmin).
7. Miller's law / chunking: split long content, forms and menus into small groups (Todaro, Soy Dalto, Polito).
8. Aesthetic-usability effect: good looks raise perceived usability (Todaro, Soy Dalto, Polito).
9. One dominant primary action per section or page, secondary actions quieter (Schoger, Sajid, Polito).
10. Test with real users and iterate (Nielsen, Polito, Kuzmin).
11. Decide from user empathy and real needs, not internal assumptions (Nielsen, Polito, Kuzmin).

## Single-author (not rules)
- Optical correction of spacing (Chainlift). C.R.A.P. framework (Showalter; note: it restates proximity, hierarchy, alignment). Max three greys (Schoger). Letter-spacing on all-caps labels (Schoger). 20-second attention window (Nielsen). Max two font families and body text at least 16px (Polito). "Typography is 80% of UI" (Sajid). Empathy beats formal frameworks (Kuzmin). No-code Figma-to-Tilda workflow (Varfolomeev).

## Real disagreements
- Formal UX research methods vs empathy-first (Kuzmin vs Nielsen/Todaro).
- Mathematically equal padding vs optical padding (Chainlift vs Showalter/Polito).
- Working memory: Polito says 3-4 items, Todaro and Soy Dalto say classic 7 plus or minus 2. Treat as: fewer is safer.
- No-code builds vs hand-coded semantic HTML/CSS (Varfolomeev vs Soy Dalto/Polito). Not relevant to our pipeline.
