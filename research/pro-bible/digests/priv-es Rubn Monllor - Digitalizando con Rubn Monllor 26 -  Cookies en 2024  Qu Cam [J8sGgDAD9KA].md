## Core thesis
For a local business with a website: publish a clear cookie policy, obtain explicit consent before setting non-essential cookies, offer a granular settings panel, and keep Accept and Reject equally prominent; some sites charge for rejecting because personalised ads fund them.

## Claims and advice
- [PRIV] Cookies are small text files storing language, login and cart data and used for tracking and ads. (4:05-5:06) — type: evidence (definition)
- [PRIV] "In January 2024 new changes came into force in Spain" requiring explicit consent before cookies and more information; no source named. (5:06-6:06) — type: opinion/unverified claim
- [PRIV] Users have the right to be informed, to consent explicitly before installation, and to delete cookies; deleting is done in browser settings. (6:06-8:09) — type: practice
- [PRIV] "Pay or consent" sites exist because personalised advertising funds news, streaming and some e-commerce sites. (8:09-10:09) — type: evidence/opinion
- [PRIV] A local business should: publish a cookie policy (purpose, who installs, how to delete) linked from the footer; get explicit consent; make the banner clear; "the Accept button can no longer be highlighted more than Reject: the buttons must be exactly the same"; allow choosing categories; secure data; review the policy regularly; use a consent-management tool or consult a privacy lawyer. (10:09-13:14) — type: practice
- [PRIV] Fines can reach "hasta 20 millones de euros o el 4% del volumen de negocio anual global"; the Spanish AEPD enforces. (13:14) — type: evidence (the 20 million or 4% cap is the GDPR maximum, not specific to cookie rules; **not verified**)
- [PRIV][ANALYTICS] Exceptions: strictly necessary cookies and cookies "used only to measure traffic or for statistical analysis" with conditions; the sentence is garbled and appears to contradict itself on consent (13:14). — type: unclear
- [PRIV] Points to the AEPD cookie guide and the European Commission for more. (14:14) — type: practice

## Techniques and steps
Cookie policy in the footer; banner with equal buttons; category settings; review regularly; get a lawyer to draft the policy.
