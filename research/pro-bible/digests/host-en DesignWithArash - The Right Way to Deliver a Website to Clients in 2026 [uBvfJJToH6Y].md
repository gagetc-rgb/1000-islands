## Core thesis
Building the site is 80% of the job; the handover is the other 20%: leave the client with a live site on their own domain, tutorials, and clear access rights.

## Claims and advice
- [HOST] A bad handover creates confusion and support email; a good one creates independence and trust. (0:00-1:01) — type: opinion
- [HOST] Publish on the client's real domain, not the platform's free subdomain; buy the plan first because it includes a free domain for one year (Wix-specific). (1:01-2:02) — type: practice (platform-specific)
- [HOST] Create a "client resource kit": short videos on editing text and images, updating blog posts, basic SEO, PDFs with screenshots. (2:02-3:04) — type: practice
- [HOST] Two ways to hand over: invite the client as collaborator with a limited Editor role (protects your design) or transfer full ownership, optionally keeping yourself as co-owner and a copy in your account. (3:04-4:06) — type: practice
- [HOST] Formula: connect plan and domain; publish on the final domain; resource kit; record tutorials; invite or transfer; send a structured final delivery email. (4:06) — type: practice
- [HOST] Do not share passwords insecurely or send loose Drive links; keep everything inside the platform. (4:06) — type: practice
