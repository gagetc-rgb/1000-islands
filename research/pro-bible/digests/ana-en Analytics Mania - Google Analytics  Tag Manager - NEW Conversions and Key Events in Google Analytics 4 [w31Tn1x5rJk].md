## Core thesis
In March 2024 Google renamed GA4 "conversions" to "key events" and reserved "conversion" for events also imported as conversions into Google Ads, to reduce mismatched numbers between the two products. Functionality did not change.

## Claims and advice
- [ANALYTICS] The flow stays the same: send an event, go to Admin > Events, mark it as a key event; metric names change, e.g. "session conversion rate" becomes "session key event rate". (0:00-1:01) — type: evidence
- [ANALYTICS] "Conversion" in GA4 now means only events that are also conversions in Google Ads; the Advertising workspace has a "conversion performance" report. (1:01-3:03) — type: evidence
- [ANALYTICS] A form submission can be both a key event and a Google Ads conversion. (5:05) — type: evidence
- [ANALYTICS] Key events are not calculated differently from legacy conversions. (5:05) — type: evidence
- [ANALYTICS] No change needed to tracking code or GTM. (4:05) — type: evidence
- [ANALYTICS] Opinion: he is "not a fan"; numbers will still differ across reports, so you must educate colleagues and clients. (3:03-4:05) — type: opinion

## Techniques and steps
Use "key events" language in reports and when you brief a client.

## Disagreements and contrarian points

## For the course
Lesson 5/6: use "key event" as the term; a short note that older tutorials and clients say "conversion". The numbers-mismatch warning is worth one sentence in a client-facing explanation.
