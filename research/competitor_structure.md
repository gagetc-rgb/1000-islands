## What to take into our structure (◇)

1. **Named lodges and boats as cards** on destination pages: the international operators sell through them. → Add a "Where you stay & sail" block to the hubs (fits question 7 about boats).
2. **Yacht landing = packages by length** (5 / 7 / 9 / 11 days) + "from $X per night, whole boat" + included list + FAQ.
3. **One call to action, repeated** after every major block ("Start planning" ×5 at Jacada).
4. **Response time as a trust point**: "Reply within 24 hours" appears at both a phinisi operator and a DMC. Ours: within 24 h, 7 days a week (owner).
5. **B2B: process + terms + credentials.** Explera shows a 4-step process, quote turnaround and licences. Nobody in the sample states **commission, white-label and client protection** openly. That is our gap to own on the travel-advisor page.
6. **Gap overall:** the international operators are broad (all of Asia), the DMCs are generic (Bali + everything), the yacht operators sell boats. Remote eastern Indonesia + honest "from" prices + clear agent terms is not covered by anyone in this sample. Small sample; treat as a hint.
