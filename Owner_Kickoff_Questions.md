# 1000 Islands Travel website — kick-off questions

Hi! Thanks again, we're starting. We want to build the site structure on real facts, not guesses, so we need your input on the points below.

- Short answers are fine. Voice messages are fine too.
- Questions marked ★ block our work, so please start with those.
- Rough numbers are OK. "About 20 trips, mostly Raja Ampat" is much better than nothing.

---

## 1. Your trips so far ★

1. ★ Roughly how many trips (bookings) did you run in 2024, 2025 and 2026 so far?
2. ★ How did they split by destination? Rough % is enough: Raja Ampat / North Sulawesi / Banda / Triton Bay / Halmahera / other.
3. ★ What format were most trips?
   - lodge or resort + speedboat days
   - private yacht or phinisi charter
   - cabins on a shared liveaboard
   - overland / mixed expedition
4. Typical party size and trip length?
5. ★ Typical price per person, plus the lowest and highest you have sold?
6. Which countries were your clients from, and how did they find you (referral, Instagram, Google, agency, returning guest)?
7. ★ Can you share a simple list of past trips? One line per trip: year, destination, number of people, client's country, how they found you, price. No names needed. It helps us pick the right journeys and prices for the site.

## 2. Destinations ★

8. ★ Which destinations have you operated yourself, and how often? (often / a few times / once / not yet)
   - Raja Ampat (north) · Misool · Triton Bay · Manado & Bunaken · Lembeh · Sangihe & Siau · Halmahera · Widi · Banda · Komodo · other
9. ★ Which 3 do you most want to sell in 2027? The same as in our interview (Raja Ampat, North Sulawesi, Banda Sea), or different?
10. Your website text has no section on North Sulawesi or Banda. Do you have notes, photos or past routes there?
11. ★ Landing pages: Raja Ampat, Private Yacht Journeys and the travel-advisor page are certain. Which 1–2 should be next: Triton Bay, North Sulawesi (Lembeh, Bunaken) or Banda Sea & Spice Islands?

## 3. Travel advisors and agencies (B2B) ★

12. How many agencies or advisors have you worked with so far, and from which countries? Are any still active?
13. ★ How do you work with them: net rates (they add their own margin) or commission? Roughly what %?
14. ★ Can you keep **one retail price for everyone**? The "from" price on the site is the same price an agent's client pays; the agent earns a commission from it (or gets a net rate). This means no extra discounts for direct clients on WhatsApp; a small gift or upgrade instead is fine.
15. ★ If an agent's client contacts you directly, will you keep the booking with the agent (or pay the agent's commission)?
16. Payment terms: deposit, balance, currency, and who collects payment from the end client?
17. Can you send documents (itinerary, vouchers) under the agency's brand, or without your logo?
18. ★ How fast can you usually reply to an agency request: a few hours, one day, two days?
19. ★ Company details we can show advisors: company type and registration, tourism licence, insurance, memberships (e.g. ASITA), years in operation.
20. Who will contact advisors (outreach): you, someone on your team, or should we plan that later?

## 4. Sample journeys ★

21. ★ Choose 4 real trips you have already run that we can turn into sample journeys. For each: route, number of days, where guests stayed or which boat, month, indicative price per person, and what kind of guests it suited.
22. Can we show "from" prices on the site (for example "from $2,400 per person")? Or do you prefer "price on request"?
23. ★ We want to show trips in 2–3 styles (for example: lodge + speedboat / premium / private yacht). Roughly what does one day cost per person in each style, in Raja Ampat?

## 5. Proof

24. Roughly how many trip photos and videos do you have? Do you own them (taken by you or your team, or by guests who gave permission)?
25. Where are your reviews: Google, TripAdvisor, WhatsApp or email messages, other? About how many?
26. Are there 2–3 past guests who would be happy to be featured in a guest story (photos + short review)?
27. ★ For the luxury trip (boats + helicopter): route, days, boat names, what made it special, and written OK from the guests to use their photos, review and Instagram link.
28. Which boats (phinisi, yachts) and other premium suppliers do you work with regularly? Can we name them on the site?
29. Team: who should appear on the site? Names, roles, photos.
