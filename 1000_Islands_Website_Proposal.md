## 1. What this project is

This is not only a website. It is a sales tool for the business. It should:

- show travel advisors and private travellers that remote Indonesia is worth the trip;
- prove that 1000 Islands Travel can deliver it smoothly ("Remote Indonesia, made easy");
- bring in enquiries: **B2B first** (travel advisors, agencies, concierge), B2C second;
- record where every enquiry comes from, so the business can count what matters most: **closed deals**.

We offer three packages. All three are fixed-price. The price does not grow if the work takes us longer than planned.

## 3. How we work: approvals and revisions

The project moves in stages. At each stage you see the result, comment, and approve it before we move on. This keeps the budget under control on both sides.

### Stage 1 — Structure and texts
We show you the site map and the text for each page. You get **up to 2 rounds of comments**, and then you approve.

### Stage 2 — Design
We show the design of the key pages (in Figma or as a clickable preview) on desktop and mobile. You get **up to 2 rounds of comments**, and then you approve.

### Stage 3 — Build
We build the site exactly as approved. When it is ready, you send **one combined list of comments**, and we fix everything on it.

### What is always free

- **Our mistakes.** Anything that does not match the approved design or text, bugs, and something broken on a phone or browser. We fix these at any stage and during the free support period after launch.
- **Factual errors in the materials you sent.** If you notice a wrong date, price or route detail, we correct it, within a reasonable number of corrections.

### What is paid

**New changes after approval** cost **IDR 350,000 per hour** (≈ $20). This includes a different button, a new colour, moving a section or changing the layout of a page you already approved. We always estimate the hours and agree with you **before** doing the work.

### To keep things fast

- One decision-maker on your side gives the final approval.
- Comments come as one combined list per round, not one message at a time.
- You reply within 3 working days. If materials or feedback are late, the launch date moves back by the same amount.

## 7. Technical SEO foundation — what it is and what it is not

**What it is.** Technical SEO makes sure Google can **find, read and correctly understand** every page, and that each page targets the searches your clients actually type. It is the foundation for any later SEO and advertising.

**What it is not.** It is **not SEO promotion**. It does not include monthly work to push the site up in Google, writing articles or building links. **Nobody can guarantee a position on Google**, and Google itself warns against anyone who promises one ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo)). A new site usually needs several months of steady content and activity to gain strong positions.

### What is included (full version: Commercial Launch and Launch + CRM)

1. **Keyword research and mapping.** We find what people actually search for, for example "Raja Ampat private yacht charter", "Lembeh muck diving trip" or "Indonesia ground handler for travel agents". Each commercial page is given its own main topic, so your pages do not compete with each other. *You receive:* a spreadsheet showing each page and the searches it targets.
2. **Clean page addresses,** for example `/destinations/raja-ampat/` and `/travel-advisors/`.
3. **Titles and descriptions** (the text people see in Google results) for every page, plus a correct heading structure. You can edit all of them in the content management system.
4. **Internal linking.** Destinations ↔ journeys ↔ experiences ↔ enquiry, so both Google and visitors find the important pages.
5. **Technical setup:**
   - XML sitemap
   - robots.txt
   - canonical tags against duplicate pages
   - HTTPS
   - correct 404 page
   - **redirects from your current site's old addresses, if you have one**, so existing Google positions are not lost
6. **Speed and mobile,** checked with Google's own tools (PageSpeed / Core Web Vitals).
7. **Images:** compressed, in modern formats, with descriptive alt text.
8. **Structured data (schema.org),** which tells Google in machine language that this is a travel company, with its name and contacts, and shows how the pages relate to each other.
9. **Sharing previews:** the correct image and title appear when a page is sent on WhatsApp, Facebook or LinkedIn. This matters when you send pages to travel advisors.
10. **Google Search Console:** connected, sitemap submitted, and indexing checked after launch.
11. **Launch SEO check:** a checklist run before and after launch, plus a baseline report for comparison later.

**Starter** includes items 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10. Keyword research is done for the main pages only.

## 8. CRM *(Launch + CRM only)*

A client database built for how a travel business actually works. Instead of scattered chats, spreadsheets and photo folders, every client has one record.

**Included**

- **Client records:** contact details, country, who they travel with, interests, how they found you (source), and marketing permission.
- **Trips linked to each client:** destination, dates, party size, amount spent, and a link to that trip's photo and video folder.
- **Enquiry pipeline:** every form submission from the website appears automatically in the CRM with its source. It then moves through the stages new → qualified → proposal sent → won / lost, kept separately for B2B and B2C.
- **Travel advisor records:** agency, contact person, the clients they sent, and the deals made.
- **Reviews and guest stories:** which guest left which review, whether permission to publish was given, and which photos can be used.
- **Follow-up reminders:** for example, ask for a review 2 weeks after a trip, or send photos and a new journey idea after 6–12 months.
- **Ready-to-use message templates** for review requests and follow-ups to past guests ("We found these photos from your Raja Ampat trip…").
- **Import of your historical clients and trips** from your existing spreadsheets, notes or exports, up to 300 records.
- **Simple dashboard:** enquiries, proposals and closed deals by month and by source.
- **Training session** plus a short written guide.

**Platform:** a proven, ready-made tool (for example Airtable), chosen together at kickoff. We aim for a free plan. If a paid plan is needed, the subscription is paid by you directly to the provider.

**Not included:** automatic face recognition in photos, automated email marketing campaigns, and integration with accounting or booking software. These can be added later as a separate project.
