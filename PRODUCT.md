## Users

- **B2B, priority #1:** boutique travel advisors, consultants and concierge companies who need a reliable local partner in remote Indonesia. Typical moment: they received an outbound email and open the site to judge whether this operator is credible enough to put their client in its hands.
- **B2C, priority #2:** upper-middle-class travellers, Australia first, couples and families, not the luxury segment. They have seen Raja Ampat and want it, but fear it is far, complicated and risky; they compare a few operators and enquire.

## Product Purpose

1000 Islands Travel sells private, tailor-made trips to remote eastern Indonesia: Raja Ampat (flagship), North Sulawesi, and whole-boat phinisi yacht charters, plus trade rates for travel advisors. The site's job is closed deals: turn visitors into enquiries (form, WhatsApp) and be the proof an advisor checks. KPI: closed deals, tracked B2B and B2C separately with source.

## Positioning

"Remote Indonesia, made easy." Extraordinary hard-to-reach places with minimum organisational headache: the operator has run these trips for about 8 years and has its own people on the ground. For advisors: "Your client remains your client. We handle Indonesia on the ground."

## Operating Context

- Trips are private and built per enquiry; prices are shown as "from $X pp" that change with trip length (days slider) across three levels (Basic, Comfort, Premium). Yachts priced per day, whole boat.
- Enquiries answered within 24 hours, 7 days a week; WhatsApp is a primary channel.
- Advisors get rates by hand after registration; no login.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fixed-price build (35m IDR). Scope = what the offer promises; the page set is fixed at structure approval, extras are paid change requests.
- Pages: Home, Raja Ampat, North Sulawesi, Yacht charters, Guest stories, About (incl. how we travel + FAQ), Travel agents, Plan your trip, two thank-you pages, legal and service pages.
- Not on the site now (wanted later, never mentioned): group tours, luxury camping, Komodo.
- Brand name collides in search with the US/Canada Thousand Islands and Jakarta's Kepulauan Seribu.
- Open: commission vs net rate, deposit %, shortest trip, yacht price unit, whether yachts sail in North Sulawesi, Google rating to show.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "1000 Islands Travel", descriptor "Remote Indonesia". Owner's lines kept on purpose: "Remote Indonesia. Off the map." and "Indonesia is easy. Until it isn't."
- Studio style direction (user, 7 Oct 2026): minimalism, simplification, strong imagery, strong design.
- Visual identity (palette, typeface) not chosen yet: it is the next stage after the grey prototype is approved.

## Evidence on Hand

The owner has 8 years of above- and underwater photos and video, yacht and helicopter trip material, and guest reviews (to be collected). No testimonials, ratings, licences or numbers have been supplied yet: everything shown in the prototype is a labelled placeholder and must not be presented as real.

## Product Principles

1. Sell the place first, then remove the fear: desire, then "made easy", then proof.
2. One clear next step everywhere; the enquiry is short and a person answers.
3. Upper-middle, not luxury: show luxury capability once, quietly.
4. B2B is never buried: the advisor path is one click from every page.
5. Fewer pages, fewer blocks, bigger imagery.
