## 2. How we would reach Australians

### A. Google Search (people who already search for the trip)

- **Targeting:** location set to Australia, English, and people searching with clear commercial intent.
- **Example keyword groups:**
  - "Raja Ampat tour / trip / liveaboard / private yacht", "Raja Ampat from Bali"
  - "Lembeh diving trip", "Bunaken diving holiday"
  - "Banda Islands tour", "Spice Islands cruise"
  - "Indonesia private yacht charter"
- **Excluded words:** cheap, budget, backpacker, homestay, jobs, volunteer, and similar low-value searches.
- **Where clicks land:** the dedicated landing pages (Raja Ampat, North Sulawesi, Banda Sea), never the homepage.

### B. Meta: Instagram and Facebook (creating demand)

- **Audience:** Australia, age ~35–65, with interests such as scuba diving, liveaboards, luxury travel, sailing and Bali.
- ◈ **"Beyond Bali" audience:** people currently travelling in Bali. Meta offers location targeting for "people travelling in this location". Offer a 4–6 day extension to Raja Ampat or Lembeh.
- **Retargeting:** show ads again to people who visited a landing page but did not enquire. This is usually the cheapest source of leads.
- **Lookalike audiences** built from the past-client list. This needs the CRM, and only includes clients who gave marketing permission.
- **Creative:** real guest photos and short videos, not stock images. This matches the strategy's "real guest proof".

### C. B2B: Australian travel advisors (the strategy's first priority)

- Build a list of Australian boutique agencies, dive-travel specialists and luxury travel advisors.
- Contact them directly (email and LinkedIn), with the Travel Advisors page as proof.
- **Costs mostly time, not ad money.** One active advisor can send several clients a year, so each signed advisor is worth far more than one B2C lead.

## 3. Cost to acquire one client — model

**Benchmark inputs:**

- ◆ WordStream 2025, Travel industry (median values, **US** search campaigns, Apr 2024 – Mar 2025): CPC **$2.12**, conversion rate **5.75%**, cost per lead **$73.70**. Source: [WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks)
  - Australia and niche keywords such as "Raja Ampat liveaboard" can cost more, because liveaboard operators bid on them.
- ◈ Meta in Australia: CPM ≈ $11–14 and CPC ≈ $0.85–1.47, according to different benchmark blogs ([Rocking Web](https://www.rockingweb.com.au/meta-ads-australia-benchmarks-2026/), [Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/australia)).
- ◈ Enquiry → booking for luxury / tailor-made travel: often quoted as 10–20%. The source is industry blogs, not audited data.

**Google Search scenarios (◇ our model):**

| Scenario | CPC | Click → enquiry | Enquiry → booking | Cost per enquiry | **Cost per booking** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pessimistic | $4.00 | 2% | 8% | $200 | **≈ $2,500** |
| Base | $2.50 | 4% | 12% | $63 | **≈ $520** |
| Optimistic | $1.50 | 6% | 20% | $25 | **≈ $125** |

**What the business can afford (◇):**

1. Revenue is about $2,000–2,500 per traveller. With the "cost × 2" pricing rule, gross margin is about $1,000–1,250 per traveller.
2. For a typical couple, that is about **$2,000–2,500 gross margin per booking**.
3. A healthy limit is roughly 25–30% of gross margin spent on acquisition, which gives **≈ $500–750 per booking**.
4. The base scenario fits within that limit. The pessimistic one does not. Only a real test will tell which scenario is true.

## 4. Test plan: first 3 months after launch

1. **Month 0:** check that tracking works end to end. Every enquiry must carry its source into the CRM or lead list. Without this, no model below can work.
2. **Months 1–3:**
   - Google Search on the 3 landing pages.
   - Meta retargeting only.
   - Plus B2B outreach to 50–100 Australian advisors.
3. **Test ad budget (◇):** at least **$500–600 per month** of ad spend.
   - At the base CPC, that is ≈ 200–240 clicks, about 8–10 enquiries and about 1 booking a month.
   - With less money, 3 months will not give enough data to judge.
   - This is more than the current total marketing budget of $500 per month, so the client has to decide.
4. **Numbers to watch each month:**
   - cost per enquiry
   - share of qualified enquiries
   - enquiry → proposal → booking rates
   - client response time (it strongly affects conversion)
5. **Stop or scale rule:**
   - Cost per booking below $750 after 3 months → scale up.
   - Above $1,500 → cut paid ads and move the money into B2B outreach and content.

## 5. How we get paid: options

| Model | How it works | For us | For the client | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Fixed retainer** | A monthly fee for managing ads, SEO and reports | Safe, predictable income | Pays even when results are weak | Our income does not grow with results |
| **2. Pay per lead** | A fixed amount for each enquiry | Medium | Pays only for enquiries | Endless arguments about "bad leads"; the client's own closing is outside our control |
| **3. Pay per closed deal** | A % of each booking we brought | Highest upside | Pays only for real results | Tracking who brought whom; long sales cycle; months with 0 income; the client's sales skills and reply speed decide the outcome, not us |
| **4. Hybrid (recommended)** | A small base fee plus a success fee per closed deal | Covers our fixed work, with upside | Low fixed cost, pays more only when earning more | Needs clear attribution rules and CRM access |

### Recommended hybrid (◇ our proposal; the numbers are for discussion)

**Base: IDR 3,000,000 per month (≈ $167).** It covers:

- technical care of the site
- management of Google and Meta ads
- monthly report
- small site improvements

**B2C success fee: 15% of the client's gross margin** on every booking from our channels (paid ads, our landing pages, our SEO pages).

- Example: a couple with $2,000–2,500 margin → $300–375 (≈ IDR 5.4–6.7m) per booking.

**B2B finder's fee: 10% of the client's gross margin** on bookings from each travel advisor we sign, for 12 months from that advisor's first booking.

**Ad budget** is paid by the client directly to Google and Meta. It never passes through us.

**Example month (◇):**

- 2 B2C bookings from ads: 2 × ~$340 ≈ $680
- plus the base fee: $167
- total ≈ **$850 per month**
- For comparison, the fixed "Growth + Ads" retainer is IDR 5.5m, about $306 per month.

### Attribution rules (must be in the agreement)

1. A booking counts as ours if the **first recorded source** in the CRM is one of our channels (UTM-tagged ads, our landing pages, or an advisor we signed), and it is booked **within 6 months** of the first enquiry.
2. Returning clients who first came through other channels, and the owner's own referrals and existing advisors, are **not** counted.
3. We get **read access to the CRM**, so both sides see the same numbers.
4. The success fee is paid **when the client receives the deposit**. If the trip is cancelled and refunded, the fee is returned.
5. The model is reviewed after 3 months, based on real numbers.

### Why the hybrid, not pure commission

- We do not control closing: reply speed, proposal quality and price are the client's side.
- A travel sales cycle can take months, so pure commission means months without income while we work.
- The base fee covers the work we certainly do. The success fee rewards the results we actually create.
- This model only works if the CRM and source tracking are built. That is an argument for the **Launch + CRM** package.
