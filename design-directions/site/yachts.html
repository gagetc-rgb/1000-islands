Why a yacht

A private yacht lets the route follow the islands rather than the roads.

Wake up beside an uninhabited island. Dive before breakfast. Move to another anchorage over lunch. Spend the afternoon in a lagoon you can only reach by boat.

We work with selected yachts and traditional Indonesian phinisi. We choose the vessel for your group and route, rather than forcing the journey around one boat.

Yacht FAQ

Whole boat or a single cabin?

We charter whole boats, so the route and the days are yours. The owner's answer on shared cabins goes here.

How many guests can a boat take?

It depends on the boat: from a couple to larger groups. Each boat above lists its cabins.

What is a day on board like?

Wake up at anchor, swim or dive before breakfast, move to the next island over lunch, and spend the afternoon in a lagoon.

Can we start and end anywhere?

Routes start and end in the ports the boats work from, such as Sorong. The owner's details go here.

Can you add helicopter transfers?

Yes, on request. Ask us when you plan the trip.
