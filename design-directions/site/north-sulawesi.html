Why go to North Sulawesi

Active volcanoes, black sand beaches and reefs over volcanic slopes, with communities that have lived beside these mountains for generations.

  • Bunaken

    Reef walls a short boat ride from Manado.

  • Sangihe & Siau

    A chain of volcanic islands between North Sulawesi and the Philippines.

  • Halmahera & Widi

    Expedition territory: volcanoes, forest and an archipelago almost without tourism.

FAQ

When is the best time to travel?

Indonesia covers an enormous area, so seasons vary between regions. We recommend a destination by the month you travel, not by one season for the whole country.

How do I get to North Sulawesi?

Fly to Manado on a domestic flight, usually through Jakarta or Makassar. From Manado we arrange the boats and transfers to every island.

Do I need to be a diver?

No. Many journeys combine snorkelling, islands, wildlife and time on land. Diving is optional unless a trip says otherwise.

What if someone gets sick or hurt?

Before every journey we plan the route around the nearest medical help and an evacuation plan. Our team on the ground is reachable 24/7.
