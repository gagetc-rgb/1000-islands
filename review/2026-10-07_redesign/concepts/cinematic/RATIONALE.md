# Home concept: Cinematic chapters

Files: `home.html`, `cinematic.css`, `cinematic.js`, `wf.js` (copy; the phone bar also hides over the closing scene). Screens in `shots/`. Owner labels: `?labels=1` or press **L**.

## The idea
The home is a short film. It opens on a title card, gives one full screen to each destination and frames the yacht differently. Quiet type-led sections follow, and it closes on a human face. Premium comes from scale, one focus per screen and image proportion, not from boxes. There are no cards, pills or crossed boxes.

## First viewport
The hero is exactly `100svh − header`. It holds the video, the title, one line and one button (Plan your trip). Along the bottom runs a **region index**: Raja Ampat / North Sulawesi / Yacht charters, each with places and "from" price, linking to its page. A minimal "Scroll" cue sits under it. Nothing of the next block shows: the hero bottom equals the viewport bottom at all six sizes. This answers the owner's doubt about R1. Nothing peeks, yet all three products and prices are on screen one.

## Hand-over to the destinations
The cue lands on **Raja Ampat**, a full-screen chapter. **North Sulawesi** has the identical anatomy: big title, one line, then a hairline credits row with places · from price · one link. These are the hero index's three fields, so the regions compare like-for-like. A small **chapter index** floats at the right edge only while the product chapters are on screen (desktop/tablet), marking the current one. A note for the real footage: match-cut the hero's underwater ending into the opening of Raja Ampat.

**Yacht charters** is a different frame: a **letterbox on black**, with title above, a full-bleed panorama and the same credits row below. It reads as another product and is the one quiet luxury moment.

## Order
Hero + index → Raja Ampat → North Sulawesi → Yacht charters (what) → Remote, made easy (the promise, right after desire peaks; 4-step timeline, no numbers) → Guest story (proof) → Reviews (unboxed quotes + "★ 4.9 on Google · 48 reviews") → For travel agents → Start with an idea (buttons, plus the person with a portrait). B2B follows the proof: an advisor checks product, process and proof before trade terms. It stays one click away in the header everywhere.

## Risks
- **Long scroll:** about 9 screens at 1440×790 vs about 5 today. Mitigated by the hero index, the chapter index, the header CTA and the phone bar.
- **Comparison:** better than separate chapters alone, but weaker than side-by-side cards for anyone who skips the hero.
- **Footage dependency:** three great full-screen stills and a panorama are required. Weak photos show more here than in a grid.
- **Phone:** the chapter index is hidden (no room); the hero index does the job. Chapter copy clears the contact bar by 88px.
- The floating chapter index is a new pattern; it needs the owner's OK.
- aman.com and blacktomato.com were used as references in spirit only and not re-verified.
