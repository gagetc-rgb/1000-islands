# Concept C · "Off the map": the archipelago is the index

## Idea
The brand is called 1000 Islands, and the buyer is afraid of one thing: "far and complicated" (◆ strategy: desire, then "made easy", then proof). So the destinations stop being three equal cards. A map of eastern Indonesia becomes the table of contents. Each offer shows where it is, its "from" price and how you get there, all on one screen.

## First viewport
Header plus a full-bleed hero video, sized `100svh − header`. Nothing from the next block peeks in. Over the video: headline, the line "Private trips to Raja Ampat and North Sulawesi", one button (Plan your trip). Phones: a three-line ~50px stack.

## Hand-over
A hairline runs along the hero's bottom edge. Under it: "Where we go ↓ · Raja Ampat · North Sulawesi · Yacht charters". That row is the only cue. A name scrolls to the map with that region already lit. The map block is exactly one screen tall, so it lands flush under the header (verified at four desktop sizes). Phones: "Where we go ↓" only.

## Where we go (one screen)
On the left: the heading and an index of the three offers. Each entry has its places, its "from" price and a "how you get there" line, e.g. "via Sorong · 1 flight from Bali" (dotted underline = placeholder). The map fills the rest of the width and runs off the right edge. Hover or focus on an entry lights its region: a solid halo, the route drawn in, the add-ons (Triton Bay, Halmahera & Widi) and a 4:5 photo of the region pinned in the empty Pacific. Yachts light both seas (◆ yachts.html). On phones: heading, then a compact map with every route drawn (no hover on touch), then three stacked cards with 3:2 photos. No carousel.

## Order and why
1. Hero
2. Where we go
3. Remote, made easy: the 4 steps on the map's dashed route line, so the process reads as a journey
4. One guest story, one screen: a 4:5 portrait with the text beside it
5. Reviews: rating line and three quotes, no boxes
6. For travel agents: a full band with an h2 and Trade rates, after the proof an advisor checks (coverage, then reviews)
7. Start with an idea: the buttons plus a portrait of the person who answers
8. Footer

## Dropped or merged
- The destination cards are merged into the map index on desktop.
- "See destinations" is replaced by the bottom-edge index row.
- The story's meta line moved under its heading (kicker ban).
- Review cards and avatars are dropped.
- The story video loop becomes a still: one moving image per page ◇.

## Creative sizes
- Hero video: full bleed. Master 16:9 3840×2160, plus its own 9:16 1080×1920 cut for phones.
- Region photos: 4:5 master 2400×3000. Shown as a ~200–260px inset on the desktop map, and as a full-width 3:2 crop on phones.
- Story photo: 4:5, its height = screen minus padding (~470×590 at 1440×790). Full width on phones.
- Portrait: 4:5 master 1200×1500, shown at 120–220px.
- Map: vector, ~56% of the width × screen height; 920:600 crop on phones. To be illustrated.

## Risks
- The map needs an illustrator, and the owner must confirm the routes. A wrong route is worse than no map.
- Region photos are postcard-sized on desktop. Desire has to come from the hero and the story ◇.
- Hover-to-light does nothing on touch, so phones get a static map.
- An advisor who skips the nav meets B2B only at block 6. "Travel agents" stays in the header.
- Block labels: hidden; `?labels=1` or press L.
