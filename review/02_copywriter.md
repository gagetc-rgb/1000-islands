## Top 10 issues (ranked)

1. **P1 · The proof pillar is missing.** The order is desire → promise → proof (◆ PLAN H2), and every page stops at the promise. "8 years" (◆ STRUCTURE_v3, PLAN §9) appears nowhere. So do the owner's credentials: "We are based in Indonesia and travel these routes ourselves" and "what can realistically be done there". Reviews and the story are placeholders, so the site asserts and never shows. Fix: one proof line each in the Home hero, the Landing §05 lead, beside the Journey form and in Plan's next steps. Say "running trips for eight years" ◆, **not** "8 years in business" (company age ◇, PLAN §8).
2. **P1 · The hero promise is demoted.** "Made easy." opens the body as an orphan fragment. "We handle… **people on the ground**" says we *handle people*. "Flights" suggests international flights; the owner covers from "arrival in Indonesia" ◆.
3. **P1 · Currency is ambiguous.** An Australian reads a bare "$X,XXX" as AUD ◆ ([AU Style Manual: Currency](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/grammar-punctuation-and-conventions/numbers-and-measurements/currency): use a prefix when the currency could be unclear). The historical figures are USD ◈ (strategy §4). The owner chooses USD or AUD. After that, every price carries the code and basis: "from US$X,XXX pp, twin share". If payment goes to an Indonesian account, mention possible bank fees ◆ ([ACCC](https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/consumers-urged-to-look-out-for-unexpected-international-transaction-fees)). Legal review is out of scope ◆ (PLAN §0a).
4. **P1 · "Agent rates" next to consumer prices undermines rate parity.** "Travel advisor? Get agent rates" appears 3× on the Landing, once right under the B2C price table. A traveller infers a cheaper price exists, which breaks the one-retail-price model ◆ (PLAN H4, §8). On Home, "One price for everyone" reads to an advisor as "no trade rate". The B2B link has 4 different labels.
5. **P1 · Unconfirmed operations are written as facts.** "24/7 support", "buffer days", "homestays", "ferry or private speedboat", "we arrange [the permit]". "24/7" also contradicts "Limited mobile signal". Only "What to expect" carries a confirm note. Tag the rest `[confirm]` so the owner doesn't approve them by silence.
6. **P2 · Hardship framing creeps in.** "Getting here is part of the journey" is the owner's Widi line (◆ PART 1), now on Raja Ampat one block before the price. "What to expect" then lists four conditions and no reassurance. That's the H2 risk ◆. Use *condition + what we do about it*.
7. **P2 · The owner's best lines were dropped.** "We do not pretend that every remote destination is luxurious." "What can realistically be done there." "For us, Indonesia has never been Bali, beach clubs and familiar tourist routes." That last one is the best hook for this audience: Australia is Bali's largest source market (◆ 23.44% of 2025 arrivals, [BPS Bali](https://bali.bps.go.id/en/news/2026/02/02/347/bali-s-foreign-arrivals-jan-dec-2025-rise--with-australia-remaining-the-largest-contributor-overall-.html)). Use it on Home (map lead or "Remote, made easy").
8. **P2 · Spelling and case are inconsistent.** "snorkeling" ×4 (Australian norm is "snorkelling", ◆ used by [Tourism Australia](https://www.australia.com/en/things-to-do/adventure-and-sports/australias-best-snorkelling-spots.html)). "Plan Your Journey" (sticky bar, nav) and "Plan your journey" (hero) are **on screen together on mobile**. Spaced em dashes everywhere (see style sheet).
9. **P2 · Landing headings carry no search words.** "Raja Ampat", "Why go", "Raja Ampat journeys". "Journeys" is brand vocabulary, not query vocabulary ◇. "Liveaboard" is the owner's own word (◆ B2B list) and is never used. Volumes not sourced ◇.
10. **P2 · Factual and consistency slips.** "Destination · West Papua": Raja Ampat has been in **Southwest Papua** since Dec 2022 ◆ ([Southwest Papua](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Papua)); Kaimana stays West Papua ◈. Misool is compared with "the north", but the section is called "Central". "8–10 days" ends at "Day 8". The footer "Regions" and the form's "Where" both include private yachts. The "Email or WhatsApp" placeholder shows only an email. Fixes are in the tables below.

## Style sheet

- **English:** Australian/British. organise, travellers, travelling, snorkelling/snorkeller, centre, neighbouring, "the other way round". Keep **enquiry/enquire** for requests (◈ AU usage, [Proofed AU](https://getproofed.com.au/writing-tips/enquiry-vs-inquiry/)).
- **Case:** sentence case for every heading, button, nav item, footer link and label. Capitals only for proper nouns. ◆ The Australian Government Style Manual prescribes sentence case for headings ([Headings](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/style-manual-resources/government-writing-handbook/editors-tips/headings)). So: "Plan your journey", "Private yachts", "For travel advisors", "How we travel". The logo lockup "1000 Islands Travel · Remote Indonesia" is the only exception.
- **Dashes:** the copy uses spaced em dashes ( — ). ◆ AU Government style uses spaced **en** dashes ( – ), not em dashes ([Dashes](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/grammar-punctuation-and-conventions/punctuation/dashes)). Pick one ◇ and halve the count: most can be full stops. Ranges: "8–10 days" ✓.
- **Ampersand:** in proper names ("Sangihe & Siau") and tags only. Use "and" in headings and prose ◇.
- **Numbers:** numerals in facts and prices ("8 days", "24 hours"), words in brand lines ("eight years") ◇.
- **Currency:** never a bare "$". Cards: "from US$4,500 pp". Write "per person, twin share" in full once per page and "Prices in US dollars" by every table (or A$ throughout). Use "pp" consistently.
- **Use:** private journey, itinerary, route, phinisi, private yacht, liveaboard (cabin), island lodge, speedboat, domestic flights, Central Raja Ampat, Southwest Papua. B2B: trade rates, commission, net rate (only once confirmed), white-label, ground handling, rate parity, "your client remains your client".
- **Avoid:** hidden gems, hidden lagoons, like nowhere else, heart of…, extraordinary, unforgettable, paradise, bespoke, "you just travel", "we handle everything" (unless the list follows), "agent rates", "24/7" (until defined), "homestay" (unless he sells them), "Thousand Islands", "1000 Islands Indonesia" (◆ PLAN H6). Use "expedition" sparingly on B2C pages: it's a hardship cue ◇.
- **CTA set** (one label per job): Explore journeys · See [place] itineraries · Plan your journey · Enquire about this journey · Send enquiry · WhatsApp us · Register as a partner · Request a trade quote · See trade terms.

## What works

1. The brand stands clear of the Thousand Islands collision: the "Remote Indonesia" lockup, the "Eastern Indonesia" eyebrow and both regions named in the first sentence ◆ (PLAN H6).
2. "What a Raja Ampat journey costs" + "we confirm the final price personally" is direct and honest ◇.
3. The diving tags and "No diving needed" in Why go are the best objection handling on the site.
4. Triton Bay is honestly labelled, and "Add Triton Bay to my trip" is an original, useful CTA.
5. Plan's "What happens next" + "same team, same reply time" is concrete, calm and in the owner's register.
