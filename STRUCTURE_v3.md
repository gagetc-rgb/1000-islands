## 2. Page by page

**1. Home**
Hero "Remote Indonesia. Off the map." + "made easy" → map: the two regions with their places → 4 featured journeys → "remote, made easy" in 4 steps → private yachts block with the luxury story → reviews → for travel advisors teaser → start with an idea (form / WhatsApp).

**2. Raja Ampat ★**
Hero → why go → sections **Central Raja Ampat**, **Misool** (photo, what is special, what you do) → **Beyond Raja Ampat: Triton Bay** (honestly labelled as a neighbouring area) → how a trip works (via Sorong) → "from" price → journeys here → short yachts block with a link to Private Yachts → best time → what to expect → reviews → FAQ → call to action + "agent rates" link.

**3. North Sulawesi ★**
Hero → why go → sections **Bunaken**, **Lembeh**, **Sangihe & Siau** → **Beyond Sulawesi: Halmahera & Widi (North Maluku)** → how a trip works (via Manado) → "from" price → journeys → best time → what to expect → reviews → FAQ → call to action + "agent rates" link.

**4. Private Yachts ★**
Sells the *format*, not the place (Raja Ampat sells the place): why a yacht ("we choose the boat around the journey") → where we sail → packages by number of days with "from" price → our boats or boat levels → luxury story → included → whole boat or a cabin → FAQ → call to action.

**5. Journeys**
Card grid: photo, destination, days, diving tag, "from" price. No "group tours coming soon" block: group tours are added with the same journey template once the first one has dates.

**6–9. Journey pages**
Facts (days, region, season, diving tag, "from" price) → highlights → route → day by day → where you stay / which boat → what can change → included → FAQ → form with this journey filled in.

**10. Stories**
3 guest stories one under another (photos, route, review, what we arranged). Luxury trip first.

**11. About**
Our story → team and people on the ground → how we travel (from enquiry to the flight home, honest about comfort) → FAQ.

**12. For Travel Advisors ★**
Hero (Become a partner · Request a trade quote) → "Indonesia is easy. Until it isn't." → what we handle → how we work with you (one price for everyone, the client stays the advisor's, white-label on request, reply within 24 hours, 7 days a week) → where we operate → proof → how a booking works → FAQ → form. Rates sent by hand after registration, no login.

**13. Plan Your Journey**
Short form (name, email / WhatsApp, dates, travellers, where, what you want, message) + WhatsApp + "we reply within 24 hours, 7 days a week". Hidden source fields for the leads list.

**Everywhere:** diving tag on journeys (*Dive-focused / Dive optional / No diving needed*) · "agent rates" link on landings and journeys · call to action at the end of every page.
